Our Story & Mission

Bringing crystal-clear perfection to pools across Malaysia since 2010

Who We Are

Founded in 2010, SunClean has grown from a small family-run business to one of Malaysia's most trusted pool maintenance companies. What began as a passion project for our founder, Tan Zhen Yang, has blossomed into a full-service pool care company serving hundreds of satisfied customers across the Klang Valley.

Our team of NSPF-certified technicians brings together decades of combined experience in pool maintenance, water chemistry, and equipment repair. We're not just pool cleaners - we're water care specialists dedicated to creating safe, beautiful aquatic environments for your family to enjoy.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional pool care services that exceed customer expectations through technical expertise, environmentally responsible practices, and unwavering commitment to quality.

Our Vision

To become Malaysia's most trusted name in pool maintenance by setting industry standards for service excellence and customer satisfaction.
